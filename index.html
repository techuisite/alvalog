Alvalog Is My New Personal Blog

June 6th, 2023

Today I am launching my new blog called.. drumroll please ..Alvalog. It is a mix of my last name Alvarez and blog. Not the most unique, but it is something I came up with on the fly one day and I am happy with it. I think the name looks cool and it is personal enough for me as a website name. I have been wanting to create a more personal blog for awhile now.

I already have a technology blog that lives on Medium, called Techuisite, but I wanted place where I could also talk about tech and anything else that interests me. I plan on sharing some of my Techuisite posts here, but I am also going to treat this as a old school blog too where I will write short and sometimes long posts about anything I want.
